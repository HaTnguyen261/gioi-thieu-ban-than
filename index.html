01 · INTRODUCTION

What Are Inventions?

An invention is a new device, method or idea created to solve a problem or make life easier. Inventions often come from people's creativity, curiosity and hard work.

💡

Creativity

Inventors use new ideas to find better ways of doing things.

🧠

Knowledge

Science and technology help turn ideas into useful inventions.

🌍

Improvement

Inventions can solve problems and improve people's lives.

Think about it!

What invention do you use every day? How would your life change without it?

02 · IMPORTANT INVENTIONS

Inventions That Changed Our Lives

Some inventions have made a huge difference in human history.

📱

Smartphones

They allow us to communicate, study, take photos and access information almost anywhere.

💻

Computers

They help people work, learn, create documents and process information quickly.

🚗

Cars

They make travelling easier and help people transport goods over long distances.

💉

Vaccines

They help protect people from many dangerous infectious diseases.

✈️

Airplanes

They connect countries and make long-distance travel much faster.

🌐

The Internet

It connects people around the world and provides access to a huge amount of information.

03 · TECHNOLOGY AROUND US

Technology in Everyday Life

Modern technology is part of our daily routines. It helps us save time, communicate and discover new things.

🎓

Education

Students can learn online, watch lessons and find useful materials.

🏥

Healthcare

Modern machines help doctors examine and treat patients.

💬

Communication

Messaging apps and video calls connect people easily.

🏠

Daily activities

Smart devices help people manage their homes and save energy.

24/7

Connected World

With the Internet and smart devices, people can communicate and share information across the world at almost any time.

04 · TWO SIDES OF TECHNOLOGY

Benefits & Challenges

Technology brings many advantages, but we should also use it responsibly.

✓ Benefits

⚡

Save time and effort.

📚

Make learning easier and more interesting.

🌏

Connect people around the world.

🩺

Improve healthcare and safety.

! Challenges
